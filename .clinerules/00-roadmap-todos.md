# Roadmap / TODO list

- `docs/roadmap.md` is the living TODO list for this repository. It is the single place
  to capture ideas, follow-ups, and possible improvements the moment they come to mind —
  they do not need to be prioritized or scheduled, just recorded.
- **Add freely.** Whenever an idea comes to mind during a task, add a bullet to
  `docs/roadmap.md` immediately instead of letting it slip away.
- **Triage at the end.** At the end of a task, update the list: mark what was done,
  remove what is stale, and keep the rest for later.
- Keep entries as short bullets; only introduce sub-sections once a group of related
  items grows large enough to need one.
